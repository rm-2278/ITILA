\begin{prob}{21.3}
Imagine fitting a mixture of ten Gaussians to data in a twenty-dimensional space. Estimate the computational cost of implementing inferences for this model by enumeration of a grid of parameter values.
\end{prob}
\begin{sol}
The number of free parameters are 20*10=200 for mean, 20*10=200 for variance and 10-1=9 for the mixing weights,
giving 409 in total. This mean if we grid search with x points per parameter, the computational cost will be $O(x^{409})$.
\end{sol}
